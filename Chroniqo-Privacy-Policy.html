This Privacy Policy explains how kdm-IT collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the Chroniqo mobile or web application, related websites, software and services (together, the "Services"). It also explains your privacy rights.

kdm-IT bv is the controller responsible for the processing described in this Policy, unless we expressly state otherwise. The Chroniqo Terms of Use govern your use of the Services.

1. Personal data we collect

Data you provide

Depending on how you use Chroniqo, we may collect:

  • Account and profile data: name, username, email address, password in hashed form, preferred language, time zone, profile image and account settings.
  • Sports and productivity data: sports, activities, schedules, goals, training records, notes, performance information and other content you enter or upload.
  • Communications: support requests, feedback, survey responses and other communications with us.
  • Subscription and transaction data: subscription type, purchase date, renewal status, price, currency, transaction identifier and limited billing information. Payments may be processed by an app store or payment provider. We generally do not receive or store complete payment-card details.
  • Content you choose to share: information you make available to a team, coach, club or another user through a sharing feature.

Please do not enter medical records, diagnoses or other sensitive personal data that Chroniqo does not request.

Data collected automatically

When you use the Services, we may automatically collect:

  • Device and technical data: device type, operating system and version, app version, browser type, language, IP address, device or app identifiers and network information.
  • Usage data: features used, screens viewed, interactions, session dates and duration, referral information and subscription events.
  • Diagnostics and security data: crash reports, performance logs, error information, authentication events and information used to detect fraud, abuse or security incidents.
  • Approximate location: a general location inferred from an IP address where needed for security, localisation, legal compliance or service operation. Chroniqo does not collect precise GPS location under the data model covered by this Policy.

We may use cookies, local storage, software development kits and similar technologies to operate the web or mobile Services, remember settings, secure accounts, measure performance and understand use. Where legally required, non-essential technologies will be used only after you provide consent through the available consent controls.

Data from third parties

We may receive:

  • purchase and subscription status from Apple App Store, Google Play or another payment provider;
  • authentication information if you choose a supported third-party sign-in method;
  • data that another user or organisation lawfully shares with you or submits about a shared activity; and
  • technical, fraud-prevention or security signals from our service providers.

If Chroniqo later connects to wearables, fitness platforms or other data sources, we will identify the data requested and obtain any permission required before importing it. This Policy must be updated before enabling the routine collection of precise location, wearable health data or additional sensitive information.

3. Sports data and sensitive information

Sports activities, performance metrics and goals are not always health data. Depending on their detail, context and use, however, they may reveal information about a person's health and may qualify as special-category personal data under the GDPR.

Where our processing of sports data is considered processing of health data or another special category, we will rely on your explicit consent or another lawful condition available under Article 9 of the GDPR. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out, but it may prevent us from providing features that require that data.

Chroniqo is a sports productivity tool and is not intended to store medical records or provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

4. How we share personal data

We may share personal data only as described below:

  • Service providers: hosting, database, security, authentication, communications, customer support, analytics, crash reporting and other vendors that process data for us under contract.
  • Payment providers and app stores: to process purchases, confirm entitlement, handle billing issues and prevent fraud.
  • People or organisations you choose: when you deliberately use a sharing, team, coach or club feature. Review the audience and settings before sharing.
  • Professional advisers: lawyers, accountants, auditors, insurers and similar advisers where reasonably necessary.
  • Authorities and legal recipients: courts, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or other parties where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety and security.
  • Business transfers: a buyer, investor or successor in connection with a merger, financing, restructuring, acquisition or sale, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards.

We require processors to protect personal data, process it only on documented instructions and assist us with applicable data-protection obligations.

We do not make private sports or account data public unless you choose to share it or disclosure is otherwise described in this Policy.

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • obtain confirmation that we process your personal data and request access to it;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • request deletion of personal data;
  • restrict certain processing;

8. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your Chroniqo account through a request by email to koen.d@telenet.be. Deleting the App from a device does not delete your account or cancel a subscription.

When an account is deleted, we delete or anonymise associated personal data in accordance with section 6, except for information we must retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes. Content previously shared with another user may remain in that user's account where they independently control it or where deletion would adversely affect the rights of others, subject to applicable law.

Account deletion does not itself cancel an Apple App Store, Google Play or other third-party subscription. Follow the cancellation process in the relevant store or account settings.

9. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access. Measures are selected based on the nature of the data, processing risks and available technology.

No system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your credentials and should notify us promptly at koen.d@telenet.be if you suspect unauthorised account access or another security issue.

10. Children

Chroniqo accounts are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly allow a child under 16 to create and control an account without any consent or authorisation required by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal data contrary to this section, contact us at koen.d@telenet.be. We will investigate and delete or otherwise handle the data as required by law.

If Chroniqo is offered to sports clubs, schools or teams for use by children, kdm-IT and the relevant organisation must establish appropriate roles, notices, permissions, safeguards and, where necessary, a data-processing agreement before that use.

11. Automated decision-making

Chroniqo does not use personal data to make solely automated decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects for users. If that changes, we will provide the information and safeguards required by law before the processing begins.

Productivity suggestions, calculated metrics or performance insights are informational features and are not used by kdm-IT to make legally or similarly significant decisions about you.
